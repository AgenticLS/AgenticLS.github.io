Schedule

December 11, 2026 · Sydney, Australia. Times are local. The program is tentative and subject to change.

The workshop features invited talks (25-min talk + 5-min Q&A), two 60-minute poster sessions, a 50-minute panel discussion, a 50-minute roundtable on lab-in-the-loop deployment bottlenecks, and two 40-minute spotlight sessions showcasing 3–4 accepted papers each.
