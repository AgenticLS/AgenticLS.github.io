Call for Papers

AgenticLS invites submissions on agentic AI systems for the life sciences — from scientific copilots to closed-loop autonomous laboratories.

Important dates

  • September 16, 2026 (AoE)
    Submission deadline — full papers and extended abstracts via OpenReview.
  • September 29, 2026 (AoE)
    Author notification — acceptance decisions released.
  • Mid-October 2026
    Camera-ready deadline — final versions of accepted papers.
  • December 11, 2026
    Workshop day — Sydney, Australia (co-located with NeurIPS 2026).
All deadlines are 11:59 PM Anywhere on Earth (AoE) on the date listed.

Overview & scope

AgenticLS focuses on agentic life-science systems as a building, deployment, and evaluation problem, with explicit attention to closed-loop discovery. We invite original, unpublished work on the design, benchmarking, and deployment of tool-using, reasoning, and multi-agent AI systems for biology and drug discovery. Submissions may span methods, systems, benchmarks, empirical studies, position papers, and applications.

Track I — Building Agentic Systems for Life Science

  • Agent architectures & reasoning patterns: LLM-based, multimodal, and retrieval-augmented agents; long-horizon planners; multi-agent scientific collaborations.
  • Harness design & orchestration: tool ecosystems and APIs for biology; multi-agent communication and routing; long-horizon memory and context management; scientific knowledge grounding.
  • Agentic literature & knowledge systems: hypothesis generation, evidence synthesis, claim verification, protocol assistance, ontology-aware reasoning, knowledge-graph-enhanced agents.
  • Generalist vs. specialist comparisons: foundation-model backbones, biology-specific instruction tuning, domain adaptation, and the role of biological foundation models inside agent pipelines.
  • Reinforcement learning for scientific agents, including RL from lab-in-the-loop feedback and reward-function design for discovery.
  • Recursive self-improvement for bio-specialized models: using general-purpose LLMs to iteratively improve specialized models through data generation/curation, automated evaluation, and feedback-driven refinement.

Track II — Closed-Loop Discovery & Applications

  • Lab-in-the-loop & robotics: closed-loop experimentation, robotic execution, active learning, adaptive experiment planning, autonomous laboratories that learn from wet-lab feedback.
  • Agents for biological design & discovery: target identification; sequence and molecular design; structure-based design; CRISPR / perturbation design; assay planning; therapeutic optimization.
  • Evaluation & benchmarks: faithfulness, reproducibility, calibrated uncertainty, expert judgment, simulator-to-lab transfer, biological validity, and system-level benchmarks.
  • Reliability, governance & biosafety: hallucinated biology, biosecurity-aware safeguards, human oversight, traceability, auditability, and deployment standards for scientific agents.

Submission instructions

Format

  • Submissions must use the NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX style file.
  • Full papers: up to 8 pages (excluding references and appendices).
  • Extended abstracts / short papers: up to 4 pages (excluding references and appendices).
  • Additional supplementary material and appendices may be included after the references; reviewers are not required to read them.
  • Submissions are anonymized (double-blind review).

Review & policy

  • Submissions are made through OpenReview.
  • Each submission receives at least three reviews; no reviewer is assigned more than three papers.
  • The workshop is non-archival: there are no published proceedings, and accepted papers retain the option to be submitted elsewhere.
  • Dual submissions are permitted, except that work already published at a prior NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR is not eligible.
  • Accepted papers will be presented as posters; a subset will be selected for spotlight presentations.
OpenReview submission portal: the submission link will be posted here once open. Please check back closer to the deadline, or watch the workshop page for updates.
